# Paasel UI copy style

Paasel is direct, useful, and warm. Clarity always comes before personality.

## Say what the action does

Use plain, active voice. Prefer **Track order** over **View details** and **Add address** over **Continue**. Keep the same action name through the full flow: **Place order** leads to **Order placed**, not a generic **Success**.

## Make recovery obvious

Errors must state what happened and the next useful action. Avoid vague or apologetic copy.

- Do: **Payment didn't go through. Try again or choose another method.**
- Don't: **Sorry, something went wrong.**

## Turn empty states into invitations

Name the empty state briefly, then offer a relevant next step. Prefer **Your cart's ready for something good** plus **Find nearby shops** over **Nothing here**.

## Sound local, not unclear

Casual, Hinglish-friendly phrasing is welcome when natural for an Indian Gen Z-adjacent audience: **Order on its way!** can feel better than corporate status copy. Screen-reader labels, errors, prices, and irreversible-action labels must remain unambiguous first and casual second.

## Accessibility checks

- Give icon-only controls a semantic label.
- Do not rely on color alone for status or errors.
- Read amounts as rupees and expose live states as **Live**.
- Keep button and confirmation verbs consistent.
